Shape

2–4 px radii. Clips, chips and inputs share the same corner. The playhead is the only 1 px vertical line that crosses a border.

Hairlines carry structure. Every region is separated by a 1 px line at #171E24, never by a shadow.

One glow only: the play button's 4 px cyan ring. Everything else is flat.

Adopted

  • Inspector never covers the frame. The current editor floats a 320 px panel on top of the video; docking it on the right frees the frame entirely.
  • Traceable numbers. Tabular mono on every duration, value and timecode means columns line up and the eye can skip.
  • Track headers make audio real. Mute, lock and add are one click, not a right-click menu.
  • Cost: the stage loses the dock width. The timeline gains a 112 px header gutter, paid for by the timeline strip getting taller.
